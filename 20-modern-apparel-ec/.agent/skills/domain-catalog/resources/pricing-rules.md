# Pricing Rules

## Core Rules
1.  **Base Price**: Every product must have a base price in JPY.
2.  **Tax**: All prices displayed to users must be tax-inclusive (10%).
3.  **Discounts**:
    *   Percentage based (e.g. 20% OFF).
    *   Fixed amount (e.g. 500 YEN OFF).
    *   Cannot result in negative price.

## Flash Sales
*   Must have `start_time` and `end_time`.
*   Must be strictly validated on checkout.
